# Contributing to Puzzary for Home Assistant

Open or choose an issue before substantial work. Keep the integration API-driven
and do not add control of Home Assistant to Puzzary core. Run
`python scripts/verify.py` and `python scripts/build_release.py` before opening a
pull request.

Contributions are submitted under Apache-2.0 as described by section 5 of the
license unless explicitly stated otherwise. Identify copied or adapted
third-party material and preserve its license and attribution.

AI tools may assist development, but the human submitter remains responsible
for understanding, testing and licensing the contribution and for checking that
generated material does not reproduce incompatible third-party code. Disclose
substantial AI assistance in the pull request when it materially shaped the
implementation.

Report vulnerabilities privately using the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
